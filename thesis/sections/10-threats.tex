\section{Limitations and threats to validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\noindent\textit{Sharpened for a technical-report audience: what can go wrong if a reader over-reads this draft.}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Prototype $\neq$ production compiler.}
    The offline parse/plan/emit/E2E path is a Hypothesis existence proof (12/12 toy schema validations).
    It is not Cascades cost-based optimisation, not Substrait wire-compatible, and not load-tested.
  \item \textbf{Literature coverage.}
    Multimodal Pass~1--5 notes exist for ACORN, VBASE, Filtered-DiskANN, FANNS survey, RRF, Condorcet, Bruch, Chen, ColBERT, PLAID, MUVERA, HyDE, MMR, BlinkDB, and Substrait/Calcite adjacency (journals 0006, 0009--0014, 0016--0019, 0025, 0031--0033).
    Other citations (Indri, BigDAWG, some vendor corners) remain \provisional{}.
    Author empiric tables throughout remain \authoronly{} / unreproduced.
  \item \textbf{ANN nondeterminism.}
    Recall is implementation- and parameter-dependent; a portable IR cannot claim bit-identical results across HNSW libraries or vendor builds \established{} as a systems fact.
  \item \textbf{Adapter impedance.}
    Not all backends expose subgraph search, native RRF, late interaction, or EXPLAIN.
    Client shims may hide latency and---if mis-marked---ACL risk; capability negotiation must fail closed \hypothesis{}.
  \item \textbf{Docs-only vendor matrix.}
    Journal 0020 cells are from public documentation (Sep~2026), not live cluster probes; APIs drift.
  \item \textbf{Synthetic FANNS is not P0.}
    Colab $N{=}50$k Gaussian smoke must not be cited as filtered-ANN quality or as an ACORN/FANNS table reproduction.
  \item \textbf{Optimism bias.}
    Algebra elegance and schema-valid plans do not guarantee planner quality without measured cost models and workloads.
  \item \textbf{External validity for RAG.}
    No generator-in-the-loop evaluation, no faithfulness/citation metrics, and no production A/B evidence are claimed.
\end{itemize}
