\subsection{Approximate nearest neighbour search}
Graph indices such as HNSW~\citep{malkov2018hnsw} and disk-oriented graphs (DiskANN line) underpin production vector search.
Search quality/latency is controlled by parameters (e.g.\ candidate list size), yielding recall--throughput curves rather than exact SQL cardinalities \established{}.

\subsection{Hybrid vector + predicate search}
Applications need ANN jointly with structured predicates.
Common strategies include \textbf{pre-filtering}, \textbf{post-filtering}, specialised low-cardinality equality indices, and \textbf{predicate-subgraph} traversal as in ACORN~\citep{patel2024acorn} \established{} (see Section~\ref{sec:related}).

\subsection{Hybrid lexical--dense fusion}
Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) combines ranked lists without score calibration~\citep{cormack2009rrf} \established{} (journal 0011).
Condorcet-fuse sorts candidates by pairwise majority over input rankings~\citep{montague2002condorcet} \established{} as the majoritarian rank-only sibling (journal 0018); Cormack AUTHORS later prefer RRF over Condorcet on their suites \provisional{} as unreproduced comparison.
Bruch et al.~\citep{bruch2023fusion} analyse the sibling \emph{convex combination} of normalised lexical and semantic scores
\(f_{\mathrm{Convex}}=\alpha\phi_{\mathrm{Sem}}(f_{\mathrm{Sem}})+(1-\alpha)\phi_{\mathrm{Lex}}(f_{\mathrm{Lex}})\)
(especially TM2C2 with theoretical min--max \(\phi_{\mathrm{tmm}}\)) versus RRF, and argue RRF is parameter-sensitive while CC is sample-efficient to tune \established{} as literature mechanisms (journal 0016); author NDCG/Recall tables are unreproduced here \provisional{}.
Chen et al.~\citep{chen2022ood} prefer RRF over tuned min--max linear interpolation in a zero-shot hybrid setting (Recall@1K; journal 0017), disagreeing with Bruch's TM2C2$>$RRF(60) NDCG claim --- an \established{} setup-level literature conflict (see \S\ref{sec:fuse-preference}).
Planner choice among \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{rrf}\), \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{condorcet}\), \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{linear}\), and \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{ltr}\) remains workload-dependent \hypothesis{}.

\subsection{Late interaction}
ColBERT~\citep{khattab2020colbert} defines \emph{late interaction}: independent encoders produce bags of contextualized embeddings \(E_q,E_d\), scored by a sum of maximum similarities (MaxSim)
\[
S_{q,d}=\sum_{i\in[|E_q|]}\max_{j\in[|E_d|]} E_{q_i}\cdot E_{d_j}^{\top}
\]
(Eq.~(3) in~\citet{khattab2020colbert}; cosine via L2-normalized dots; squared L2 also evaluated) \established{} (multimodal journal 0012).
Document bags are offline-indexable; MaxSim is pruning-friendly for end-to-end retrieval.
Subsequent engines add physical rewrites: PLAID's centroid interaction and multi-stage centroid pruning funnel (candidate generation $\rightarrow$ pruned centroid MaxSim $\rightarrow$ unpruned centroid MaxSim $\rightarrow$ residual MaxSim)~\citep{santhanam2022plaid} \established{} as literature mechanisms (journal 0014; author latency tables unreproduced), and MUVERA Fixed Dimensional Encodings that reduce Chamfer/MaxSim search to single-vector MIPS plus MaxSim rerank~\citep{jayaram2024muvera} \established{} as a literature mechanism (journal 0013); RQL compile packaging of the ColBERT$\rightarrow$PLAID$\rightarrow$MUVERA ladder remains \hypothesis{}.
Separately, HyDE~\citep{gao2023hyde} \established{} zero-shot dense retrieval by generating a hypothetical document with an instruction-following LM and retrieving neighbors of its Contriever embedding in document space (journal 0032); RQL \texttt{REWRITE HYDE} packaging remains \hypothesis{}.
Carbonell and Goldstein~\citep{carbonell1998mmr} \established{} Maximal Marginal Relevance ($\lambda$-relevance vs novelty) for diversity reranking and anti-redundant summarization (journal 0033); RQL \texttt{DIVERSIFY MMR} packaging remains \hypothesis{}.

\subsection{RAG as a staged pipeline}
A typical RAG retrieve path is: embed $\rightarrow$ search channels $\rightarrow$ filter $\rightarrow$ fuse $\rightarrow$ (rerank) $\rightarrow$ expand/traverse $\rightarrow$ generate.
RQL aims to name these stages as plan operators rather than ad-hoc functions \hypothesis{}.
