\section{RQL algebra (original sketch)}
\label{sec:algebra}

\noindent\textit{All operators in this section are \hypothesis{} unless marked otherwise.}

\subsection{Typed evidence relation}
An \textbf{evidence relation} $E$ is a multiset of tuples with at least:
\begin{align*}
E \subseteq\ &\mathit{Id} \times \mathit{Payload} \times \mathit{Score} \times \mathit{Channel}\\
&\times \mathit{Provenance} \times \mathit{Attr}^* .
\end{align*}
Informal signature: each tuple carries a channel-local score until a fuse operator.
Mandatory ACL attributes (tenant, clearance, \ldots) are part of $\mathit{Attr}$ and must remain enforceable in every physical plan \hypothesis{}.

\paragraph{Channels.}
$\mathit{Channel}\in\{\mathsf{dense},\mathsf{bm25},\mathsf{late},\mathsf{graph},\mathsf{fused},\ldots\}$.
Scores are comparable \emph{within} a channel; cross-channel comparison requires $\mathrm{Fuse}$.

\subsection{Rewrite examples (hypothesis, literature-grounded)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Post-filter brittleness (ACORN seed).}
    $\mathrm{Filter}(P)\circ \mathrm{Search}_{dense}(q,k)$ may be rewritten toward a physical $\mathrm{SUBGRAPH}$ or over-fetched $\mathrm{POST}$ plan when estimated selectivity / query--predicate correlation is adverse~\citep{patel2024acorn} \hypothesis{} (choice is physical; logical $\mathrm{Filter}$ remains).
  \item \textbf{Iterator pushdown (VBASE seed).}
    When the adapter exposes Volcano-style ANN iteration, $\mathrm{Filter}(P)\circ \mathrm{Search}_{dense}$ may compile to $\mathrm{FilterExec}=\mathrm{ITERATIVE}$ (filter during $\mathrm{Next}$) rather than TopK-then-drop~\citep{zhang2023vbase} \hypothesis{}.
  \item \textbf{Label-specialized graphs (Filtered-DiskANN seed).}
    Equality/label predicates with a capable filtered graph may compile to $\mathrm{SPECIALIZED}$~\citep{gollapudi2023filtered} \hypothesis{}; workload-stable partitions may compile to $\mathrm{PARTITION}$ and multi-algorithm backends to $\mathrm{ROUTER}$~\citep{lin2025fanns} \hypothesis{}; unsupported predicate shapes must not silently use specialised paths.
  \item \textbf{Hybrid fuse (RRF / linear / LTR).}
    Parallel \(\mathrm{Search}_{dense}\) and \(\mathrm{Search}_{bm25}\) (or other channels) remain separate until a fuse op.
    \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{rrf}(k_{\mathrm{rrf}})\) with default \(k_{\mathrm{rrf}}=60\)~\citep{cormack2009rrf} is the portable \emph{rank-only} default when scores are incomparable \established{} (journal 0011).
    \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{condorcet}\) is the majoritarian rank-only sibling (pairwise-majority sort)~\citep{montague2002condorcet} \established{} as mechanism (journal 0018); offer when pairwise Condorcet semantics are desired, without displacing RRF as default \hypothesis{} (Cormack AUTHORS prefer RRF on their suites).
    \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{linear}(\alpha)\) follows Bruch-style convex combination of normalised scores~\citep{bruch2023fusion} \established{} as mechanism (journal 0016; unreproduced NDCG \provisional{}); escalate to \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{ltr}\) when a learned multi-feature model is justified \hypothesis{}.
    Choosing among \(\{rrf,condorcet,linear,ltr\}\) is physical/policy \hypothesis{} (\S\ref{sec:fuse-preference}; Chen$\leftrightarrow$Bruch setup disagreement \established{}, journals 0016--0017).
  \item \textbf{Late-interaction leaf + rewrites (ColBERT / PLAID / MUVERA).}
    Logical $\mathrm{Search}_{late}$ carries MaxSim-sum semantics~\citep{khattab2020colbert} \established{}.
    Physical compile targets \hypothesis{}: (i) native multi-vector MaxSim; (ii) PLAID-class centroid interaction + multi-stage prune then residual MaxSim~\citep{santhanam2022plaid} \established{} as the literature pipeline (journal 0014; unreproduced speedups remain \provisional{}); (iii) MUVERA FDE$\rightarrow$MIPS then MaxSim/Chamfer rerank~\citep{jayaram2024muvera} when only single-vector ANN is available (journal 0013).
    Missing multi-vector, PLAID, and FDE capabilities $\rightarrow$ refuse or EXPLAIN, not silent dense cosine substitution.
  \item \textbf{Similarity join.}
    $\mathrm{VSimJoin}_{\theta}(E_1,E_2)$ may compile to nested $\mathrm{Search}_{dense}$ / range-iterator joins when $\mathrm{ITERATIVE}$ (or equivalent) exists~\citep{zhang2023vbase} \hypothesis{}; otherwise to blocked nested loops with explicit cost --- not claimed implemented.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Semantics sketch}
Channels remain separate until fuse.
Mandatory predicates in $P_{\mathrm{acl}}\subseteq P$ must be enforced by physical plans that cannot strip them client-side \hypothesis{}.
Rewrite operators carry explicit token/latency cost attributes for the optimiser.

\subsection{Delineation from survey material}
RRF~\citep{cormack2009rrf} (formula + \(k{=}60\); journal 0011), Condorcet-fuse~\citep{montague2002condorcet} (journal 0018), Bruch CC/TM2C2 linear fusion~\citep{bruch2023fusion} (journal 0016), Chen zero-shot RRF-vs-interp~\citep{chen2022ood} (journal 0017), ColBERT MaxSim/late interaction~\citep{khattab2020colbert} (journal 0012), PLAID centroid interaction/pruning~\citep{santhanam2022plaid} (journal 0014), MUVERA FDE reduction~\citep{jayaram2024muvera} (journal 0013), HNSW~\citep{malkov2018hnsw}, ACORN's predicate-subgraph idea~\citep{patel2024acorn}, VBASE's relaxed-monotonicity iterators~\citep{zhang2023vbase}, and Filtered-DiskANN's label-aware graphs~\citep{gollapudi2023filtered} are prior art (multimodal notes: journals 0006, 0009--0014, 0016--0018).
\textbf{Original to this draft:} packaging them as a single portable retrieval IR with explicit physical filter modes and budget contracts aimed at RAG reliability \hypothesis{}.

\subsection{Draft LogicalPlan schema freeze (Hypothesis IR)}
\label{sec:logical-schema}
A machine-checkable JSON Schema (draft 2020-12) for \texttt{LogicalPlan} is frozen at package path \texttt{schemas/logical-plan.schema.json} (version \texttt{0.1.0-draft}; journal 0021).
It encodes the kernel operators above plus optional capability annotations aligned to the Sep~2026 vendor matrix (\S\ref{sec:vendor-matrix}).
This freeze is \hypothesis{} IR packaging---\textbf{not} a published standard---and does not promote unreproduced AUTHOR metrics.
Validated examples live under \texttt{schemas/examples/}; the harness \texttt{experiments/harness/validate\_plans.py} checks them with \texttt{jsonschema}.
