\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{Problem}
Modern RAG stacks must retrieve \emph{evidence}---chunks, passages, entities, paths---under mixed modalities: dense vectors, sparse/BM25 channels, structured predicates (including ACLs), and increasingly late-interaction or graph expansion~\citep{lewis2020rag}.
Vector databases expose powerful but \textbf{non-portable} interfaces (JSON filters, GraphQL operators, SQL extensions, Redis commands, Cypher search).
As a result, accuracy, latency, and reliability become properties of brittle Python glue rather than of inspectable plans.

\subsection{Motivation (accuracy, latency, reliability)}
\begin{description}
  \item[Accuracy.] Hybrid fusion (e.g.\ reciprocal rank fusion~\citep{cormack2009rrf}), over-fetch+rerank, and multi-query rewriting improve recall only when stages are explicit and tunable.
  \item[Latency.] Filter placement (pre vs.\ post vs.\ subgraph vs.\ specialised label graphs vs.\ iterator ANN) and ANN parameters dominate end-to-end cost~\citep{patel2024acorn,gollapudi2023filtered,zhang2023vbase}.
  \item[Reliability.] Silent empty results, client-side ACL filtering, and irreproducible \texttt{efSearch} folklore are operational failures; plans with \texttt{EXPLAIN} and mandatory predicate pushdown targets are the remedy \hypothesis{}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Thesis statement}
\label{sec:thesis-stmt}
\textbf{RQL is a portable IR for retrieval plans}---an algebra over scored multisets of evidence together with a Cascades/Calcite-style physical planner that chooses filtered-ANN and fusion strategies under explicit recall/latency/ACL contracts, then compiles via polystore-like shims to concrete engines.
The textual DSL is a frontend, not the contribution's centre of mass \hypothesis{}.
