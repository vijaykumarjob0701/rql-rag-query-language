\begin{abstract}
Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems compose embedding search, lexical retrieval, metadata and ACL filters, hybrid fusion, reranking, and sometimes graph or multi-hop expansion.
Today those stages are usually glued together in application code against vendor-specific vector-database APIs.
This draft proposes \textbf{RQL (Retrieval Query Language)} not merely as ``SQL for vectors'', but as a \textbf{portable intermediate representation for retrieval plans}: a small algebra over scored evidence, plus cost-based physical planning under recall, latency, and ACL constraints, compiling through adapters to heterogeneous backends.

We separate \established{} survey material from \hypothesis{} original design and from \provisional{} literature pending multimodal re-read.
Careful multimodal passes over ACORN~\citep{patel2024acorn}, VBASE~\citep{zhang2023vbase}, and Filtered-DiskANN~\citep{gollapudi2023filtered} ground filtered-ANN physical strategies (\texttt{SUBGRAPH}, \texttt{ITERATIVE}, \texttt{SPECIALIZED}); Cormack et al.~\citep{cormack2009rrf} grounds \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{rrf}\); Bruch et al.~\citep{bruch2023fusion} ground \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{linear}\) / CC--vs--RRF delineation; Chen et al.~\citep{chen2022ood} ground the zero-shot RRF-vs-linear-interp critique that resolves the Bruch disagreement as a setup conflict; ColBERT~\citep{khattab2020colbert} grounds MaxSim/late-interaction scoring for \(\mathrm{Search}_{late}\); PLAID~\citep{santhanam2022plaid} grounds centroid-interaction / multi-stage pruning for late-interaction engines; MUVERA~\citep{jayaram2024muvera} grounds an FDE$\rightarrow$MIPS$+$MaxSim-rerank physical rewrite (journals 0011--0014, 0016--0017).
We define an evaluation \emph{plan} and reproducibility layout; we do \textbf{not} report unreproduced benchmark scores.
\end{abstract}
