\begin{abstract}
Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems compose embedding search, lexical retrieval, metadata and ACL filters, hybrid fusion, reranking, and sometimes graph or multi-hop expansion.
Today those stages are usually glued together in application code against vendor-specific vector-database APIs.
This draft proposes \textbf{RQL (Retrieval Query Language)} not merely as ``SQL for vectors'', but as a \textbf{portable intermediate representation for retrieval plans}: a small algebra over scored evidence, plus cost-based physical planning under recall, latency, and ACL constraints, compiling through adapters to heterogeneous backends.

We separate \established{} survey material from \hypothesis{} original design and from \provisional{} literature pending multimodal re-read.
Careful multimodal passes over the FANNS survey of Lin et al.~\citep{lin2025fanns} (VSP/VJP/SJP/SSP; A1--A17), ACORN~\citep{patel2024acorn}, VBASE~\citep{zhang2023vbase}, and Filtered-DiskANN~\citep{gollapudi2023filtered} ground filtered-ANN physical strategies (\texttt{PRE}/\texttt{POST}/\texttt{SUBGRAPH}/\texttt{ITERATIVE}/\texttt{SPECIALIZED}/\texttt{PARTITION}/\texttt{ROUTER}); Cormack et al.~\citep{cormack2009rrf} grounds \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{rrf}\); Montague and Aslam~\citep{montague2002condorcet} ground \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{condorcet}\) (pairwise-majority sort); Bruch et al.~\citep{bruch2023fusion} ground \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{linear}\) / CC--vs--RRF delineation; Chen et al.~\citep{chen2022ood} ground the zero-shot RRF-vs-linear-interp critique that resolves the Bruch disagreement as a setup conflict; ColBERT~\citep{khattab2020colbert} grounds MaxSim/late-interaction scoring for \(\mathrm{Search}_{late}\); PLAID~\citep{santhanam2022plaid} grounds centroid-interaction / multi-stage pruning for late-interaction engines; MUVERA~\citep{jayaram2024muvera} grounds an FDE$\rightarrow$MIPS$+$MaxSim-rerank physical rewrite (journals 0011--0014, 0016--0019).
A docs-only vendor API matrix (journal 0020) \established{} the adapter surfaces RQL must compile to.
A draft LogicalPlan/PhysicalPlan JSON Schema (\texttt{0.1.0-draft}; journal 0021) freezes that IR as \hypothesis{} packaging---not a standard; a toy textual RQL parser (journal 0022) prototypes the frontend onto that IR for a tiny subset; a deterministic Logical$\rightarrow$Physical planner stub (journal 0023) applies docs-derived capability profiles to emit schema-valid PhysicalPlans; an adapter emit stub (journal 0024) turns a subset of those plans into docs-shaped Qdrant/ES/pgvector request sketches without live DB calls; a Substrait/Calcite adjacency pass (journal 0025) \established{} portable-plan and optimizer-framework facts while keeping RQL packaging \hypothesis{} (inspired-by, not an implementation); an offline end-to-end CLI (journal 0026) glues parse$\rightarrow$plan$\rightarrow$emit over toy examples without live DB calls.
We define an evaluation \emph{plan} and reproducibility layout; we do \textbf{not} report unreproduced benchmark scores.
\end{abstract}
