\section{Conclusion and next steps}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We framed RQL as a \textbf{portable retrieval-plan IR} for RAG---algebra plus physical planning under recall/latency/ACL---rather than as cosmetic SQL for vectors \hypothesis{}.
Established priors ground the pieces: FANNS filter strategies~\citep{lin2025fanns,patel2024acorn,zhang2023vbase,gollapudi2023filtered}; Fuse-family mechanisms~\citep{cormack2009rrf,montague2002condorcet,bruch2023fusion,chen2022ood}; late-interaction ladders~\citep{khattab2020colbert,santhanam2022plaid,jayaram2024muvera}; HyDE rewrite~\citep{gao2023hyde}; MMR diversify~\citep{carbonell1998mmr}; BlinkDB-style contracts~\citep{agarwal2013blinkdb}; Substrait/Calcite adjacency~\citep{substrait,begoli2018calcite}.
\textbf{Original packaging} is the single IR, capability-negotiated compile stack, and honesty discipline---not the underlying IR formulas themselves.

Feasibility evidence on disk: draft schemas \texttt{0.1.0-draft}, docs-only vendor matrix, toy parser/planner/emit, and offline E2E \textbf{12/12} schema-validated runs.
Colab synthetic FANNS remains plumbing only.
