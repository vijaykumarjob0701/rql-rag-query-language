## Abstract (paraphrase)

Dedicated survey of **filtered ANN (FANNS)** over vector–scalar hybrid data. Formalises hybrid dataset / hybrid query and evaluation metrics (incl. selectivity). Proposes a **pruning-focused** classification into **VSP, VJP, SJP, SSP**, summarising 17 algorithms more finely than pre-/post-/in-filtering. Reviews hybrid datasets and analyses query difficulty via **distribution** relationships (ID/POD/OOD) in addition to selectivity. Aims at shared vocabulary and practitioner guidance; releases dataset/difficulty code.
