## Abstract (paraphrase)

Filtered ANNS retrieves neighbors among points matching query labels. Prior methods mostly tweak search, not graph construction. **FilteredVamana** (streaming) and **StitchedVamana** (batch stitch of per-label Vamana graphs) build label-aware navigable graphs; authors report large efficiency/accuracy gains vs post/inline baselines, including SSD settings.
