## Abstract (paraphrase)

Study of hybrid lexical+semantic fusion via **convex combination (CC)** of scores and **RRF**. Authors report RRF is parameter-sensitive; CC learning is largely agnostic among monotone normalizations; CC (esp. TM2C2) outperforms RRF in-domain and out-of-domain on their suite; CC tunes sample-efficiently.
