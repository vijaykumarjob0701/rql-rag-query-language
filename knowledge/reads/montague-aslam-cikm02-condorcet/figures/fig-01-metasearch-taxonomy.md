# Figure 1

**Caption (paper):** Metasearch techniques may or may not require relevance scores, and may or may not require training. Listed are metasearch algorithms that we shall discuss.

**2×2 grid (viewed):**
| | no training | training |
|---|---|---|
| **ranks only** | Condorcet-fuse; Borda-fuse; rCombMNZ | Weighted Condorcet-fuse; Weighted rCombMNZ |
| **relevance scores** | CombMNZ | Weighted CombMNZ |

**RQL use:** Classifies Fuse family siblings by input requirements — \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{condorcet}\) / \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{rrf}\) are ranks-only; \(\mathrm{Fuse}_{linear}\) is score-side (CombMNZ/CC lineage).
