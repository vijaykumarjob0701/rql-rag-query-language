## Abstract (paraphrase)

Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) combines document rankings from multiple IR systems with a simple reciprocal-rank sum. Authors report it consistently beats individual systems and Condorcet Fuse on TREC metaranking sets, and that fusing LETOR 3 baseline learners yields a strong unsupervised meta-ranker.
